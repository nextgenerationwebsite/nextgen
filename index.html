Who We Are

About NextGen Enrichment

Next Gen Enrichment is a youth-led nonprofit created by students who noticed a gap in mentorship access for kids in San Diego. We mentor young students in sports and music to help them build confidence, skills, and community.

Funds support San Diego organizations serving underserved youth, giving more kids the chance to play, create, and grow.
